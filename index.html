Johannes Stümpfle

PhD Candidate @University of Stuttgart

🥐 I do like croissants!

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Stuttgart with a background in electrical engineering. I am interested in intelligent information processing based on machine learning and deep learning. Currently, my research focuses on generative AI and how it can be leveraged for variability-intensive software systems, exploring the exciting semantic capabilities of these models. As part of this research, I recently spent time as a visiting researcher at Inria in Lille France.

Away from the keyboard (the computer one), I make music, some of it might end up on SoundCloud.
